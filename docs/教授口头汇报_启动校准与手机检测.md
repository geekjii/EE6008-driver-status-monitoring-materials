# 教授口头汇报：启动校准与手机检测

英文界面地址：`http://127.0.0.1:8000/?lang=en`。启动方式和原来相同，双击项目根目录中的 `启动疲劳监测.command`。右上角可以切换 English / 中文。

## 可以直接说的英文（约一分钟）

For this update, I’ve added two features to the local prototype: startup calibration and phone detection.

At startup, the user faces the camera with their eyes open and mouth closed for about five seconds. The system uses this to establish their normal eye and mouth baseline and set personal detection thresholds. This accounts for differences between people. If the face is lost or turned away, the timer pauses. Monitoring begins once calibration is complete.

For phone detection, I’m using a pretrained YOLO26n model. After calibration, it checks for phones about every 0.35 seconds and shows a bounding box and confidence score. If detections persist for about two seconds, it triggers a warning. Basic tests with a phone held in front of the body or near the ear have worked.

It currently detects phone presence, so a phone on a desk may also trigger an alert. The next step is to combine phone location with hand position and head direction to better identify actual phone use.

## 中文理解

第一项是在开始监测时，让用户正对镜头、正常睁眼和闭嘴，收集约五秒的正常状态。因为每个人眼睛和嘴巴的比例不同，系统先了解“你正常时是什么样”，再生成适合你的闭眼、张嘴阈值。人脸丢失或明显转头时暂停校准计时，避免把无效画面算进去。

第二项是已经接入预训练 YOLO26n 手机模型，检测后显示框和置信度。校准完成后约每 0.35 秒检查一次；持续检测约两秒才提醒。胸前拿手机、手机放耳边等基础场景已有测试，但小手机、遮挡或移出画面仍会漏检。当前还不能确认用户是否正在使用手机，桌面手机也可能触发提醒。

## 教授追问时的简短回答

**Why do you need the five-second calibration?**

People have different eye and mouth proportions, so a single fixed threshold may not suit everyone. The calibration provides a personal baseline for setting the thresholds.

**What exactly do you calculate during calibration?**

We calculate the Eye Aspect Ratio, or EAR, and the Mouth Aspect Ratio, or MAR, from facial landmarks. We collect at least 30 valid samples over about five seconds of valid observations. We use the 75th percentile of EAR and the 25th percentile of MAR to reduce the influence of brief blinks or smiles. The eye threshold is 72 percent of the eye baseline, and the mouth threshold is 2.2 times the mouth baseline, with limits to keep the values within a configured range.

**Does calibration prove the person is alert?**

No. We ask the user to begin with their eyes open and mouth closed. The calibration estimates their normal facial measurements; it does not independently verify that they are alert.

**Did you train the phone model yourself?**

No. I’m using a pretrained YOLO26n model and selecting the cell-phone class. I’ve integrated it into our monitoring pipeline, but I haven’t trained a custom driving-specific model yet.

**What does the confidence score mean?**

It is the model’s confidence in that detection. It is not the overall accuracy of our system. We currently use a detection threshold of 0.35, but we still need broader tests to evaluate false alarms and missed detections.

**Why wait two seconds before warning?**

It reduces alerts from brief appearances or isolated detections. The phone state is updated periodically, and the timer resets when the next check no longer detects a phone.

**Can it tell whether the driver is using the phone?**

Not reliably yet. It detects a phone in the image. We still need hand and face relationships to distinguish active use from a phone that is simply visible.

## 演示时的顺序

1. 切换到 English，点击 **Start monitoring**，正对镜头、正常睁眼和闭嘴。
2. 指着 **Personal calibration** 的剩余时间和样本数，说明约五秒有效画面用于建立个人基准。
3. 校准完成后，指着 EAR / MAR 下方生成的阈值。
4. 将手机清楚地放入画面并保持约两秒，展示手机框、置信度和 **Sustained phone detection** 提醒。
5. 移开手机，说明状态会经过恢复确认后回到正常。

以上数字来自当前配置和实现。基础场景测试用于展示功能；目前没有足够的标注数据支持整体准确率或驾驶场景可靠性数字。
